This module shows an image next to each record in the records panel of
geoengine views, for example the logo of a company or region next to its name,
and at the top of the info box that opens when a feature is clicked.

A vector layer can also draw the image on its polygons: repeated as their
background, or once in their middle.

The image field is chosen per geoengine view. Records without an image show
only their name.
