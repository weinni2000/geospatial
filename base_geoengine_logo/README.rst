This module shows an image next to each record in the records panel of
geoengine views, for example the logo of a company or region next to its
name, and at the top of the info box that opens when a feature is
clicked.

A vector layer can also draw the image on its polygons: repeated as
their background, or once in their middle.

The image field is chosen per geoengine view. Records without an image
show only their name.

Configuration
=============

1. Go to *GeoEngine > Configuration > View Management* and open a
   geoengine view.

2. On the *GeoEngine Data* tab, set *Record Image Field* to an image
   field of the view's model (e.g. *Image* of contacts).

3. To draw the image on the polygons, open a vector layer of the view
   and set *Record Image on Polygons* to *Background (tiled)* or
   *Centered*.

   *Centered* draws the image once per polygon, on the point farthest
   from its edges (of the largest part of a multipolygon). At every zoom
   level it uses the largest height between *Maximum Image Height* and
   *Minimum Image Height* at which at least *Minimum Visible Share* of
   the image lies inside the polygon; when even the minimum height
   doesn't fit, the image appears only after zooming in.

A module can also set it in XML:

.. code:: xml

   <record id="my_geoengine_view" model="ir.ui.view">
       <field
           name="geoengine_record_image_field_id"
           ref="my_module.field_my_model__image_128"
       />
   </record>
